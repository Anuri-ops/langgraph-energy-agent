FAULT DIAGNOSIS GUIDE - COMMON PERFORMANCE ISSUES
Sudden output drop of more than 10 percent: check for soiling, shading, inverter faults, or grid curtailment.
Gradual output decline over weeks: likely panel degradation, module soiling accumulation, or gearbox wear in wind assets.
Intermittent output: often caused by loose DC connections, faulty sensors, or communication dropouts between SCADA and the asset.
Zero output from a single asset while neighbours operate normally: check the inverter status and the combiner box fuses first.
